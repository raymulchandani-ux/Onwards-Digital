Every new client starts with a 15-minute consultation

It's free and it happens before we book the service, either in the chair or on the phone. We look at your hair, talk about what it does on a Tuesday, and agree on a plan and a price. For colour, bring a photo or two of what you like — and one of what you don't.

24-hour cancellation policy

Cancel or move your appointment up to 24 hours before and there's no charge. Inside 24 hours we charge half the booked service; no-shows are charged in full. Colour appointments hold a chair for three hours, so we hold to this one.

Parking is behind the building

Turn in from Gibson Street; the lot has six spaces marked Tessaline and the back door is unlocked during hours. Street parking on South Congress is metered and mostly full by ten, so use the lot.

What to arrive with

For curly cuts, come with your hair down, dry, and styled the way you normally wear it. For everything else, arrive as you are. Coffee and sparkling water are on us; the espresso machine is the loud thing in the corner.

Running late, or need to reschedule?

Call or text (512) 555-0134. More than 15 minutes late and we may need to shorten the service or move you to the next opening.

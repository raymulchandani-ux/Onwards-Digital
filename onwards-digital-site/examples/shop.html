The shop

A small shop, kept by someone who reads everything in it.

Wren opened Quillmont in the spring of 2016 with eleven hundred books, a borrowed till and a rule that still holds: nothing goes on the shelf that somebody here hasn't read and would press into your hands. That's why the fiction wall is deep rather than wide, why the poetry section is bigger than a town this size has any right to, and why the nature-writing table by the window is the first thing you see.

The paper came a year later, when Wren noticed customers asking where they could buy a decent notebook to go with the book they'd just bought. Now there's a whole wall of it — notebooks bound in cloth, fountain pens you can try before you buy, and letterpress cards printed a mile away in the River Arts District.

We don't stock a book because it's selling. We stock it because we'd be embarrassed if you asked and we didn't have it.

— Wren

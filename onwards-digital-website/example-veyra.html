Questions

The short answers.

Does Veyra hold my money?

No. Your account stays with your broker, in your name. Veyra has permission to place trades in it, and nothing else. You can revoke that permission from your broker's side at any time.

What if I disagree with a trade?

Every order is visible before it fills, with a short note on the reasoning. You can pause the model, skip a single trade, or cap any position. If you skip a lot, the model will tell you your setting probably isn't right.

Is this advice?

No. Veyra is a tool that executes a model's decisions in your account at your instruction. It doesn't know your situation and isn't a substitute for someone who does. You can lose money, including money you'd rather not.

How do I cancel?

One tap in settings. Copying stops immediately; your positions stay where they are, yours to keep or close.

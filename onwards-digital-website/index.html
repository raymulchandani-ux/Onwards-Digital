Questions people ask

05 — Questions
What happens after I send the free mockup form?

We read it, look at your current site if you have one, and design your homepage. You get it by email within 3–5 business days as a picture of the page at phone and desktop sizes. There’s nothing to pay and nothing to cancel.

Do I need to write the words?

No. Tell us the facts — what you do, prices, hours, what customers ask — and we write the copy. You’ll edit it during revisions until it sounds like you.

What if I don’t have good photos?

We design so that phone photos taken in daylight look good, and we’ll tell you exactly which four or five shots to take. If you’d rather, we can source licensed stock photography for the launch and swap in your own later.

Who owns the website?

You do. The code, the domain, the content — from day one. Monthly Care only adds a 24/7 support system; the website is still yours.

Can I take bookings or payments?

Yes — all plans include a booking or payment integration (a reservation system, an appointment tool, or a simple online-ordering link). Tell us what you already use and we’ll connect it.

What does Monthly Care actually cover?

An editor, so you can change menus, hours, prices and photos yourself whenever you like, plus support by email. We’ll make up to three a month for you.

I’m not in the US. Does that matter?

No. We work by email and everything is online. Prices are in US dollars; switch the toggle at the top of the page to see them in AED.

The family

Two generations, one pasta table.

Our parents opened Ossobello in the spring of 1994 with a second-hand wood oven and a menu written on butcher paper.

Giuseppe came from Mantua, where tortelli di zucca is Sunday lunch. Anna is from the Hill, three streets over. The room you sit in was a shoe repair shop. The oven has never moved.

We took the kitchen in 2016. Rosa rolls the pasta every morning at seven on the same board our father used. Marco runs the floor and buys the wine. Our mother still makes the polpette on Sundays and will tell you if you're eating too fast.

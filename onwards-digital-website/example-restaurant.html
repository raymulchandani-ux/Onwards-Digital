The family

Two generations, one pasta table.

Our parents, Giuseppe and Anna, opened Ossobello in the spring of 1994 with a second-hand wood oven and a menu written on butcher paper.

Giuseppe came from Mantua, where tortelli di zucca is Sunday lunch. Anna is from Federal Hill, three streets over. The room you sit in was a shoe repair shop; the oven has never moved.

We took over the kitchen in 2016. Rosa rolls the pasta every morning at seven. Marco runs the floor and buys the wine, most of it from small growers in Lombardy and Piedmont, a few from friends further south. Our mother still makes the polpette on Sundays and will tell you if you're eating too fast.

Rosa & Marco BellandiOwners

Private dining & the occasional letter

The back room seats twenty-two for family dinners and rehearsal suppers; ask for Marco. We write a few times a year about truffle season, the August closure and the odd wine dinner.

The paper wall

Half the shop is for writing things down.

We started stocking paper because people kept asking where to buy a decent notebook to go with the book they'd just bought. Now it's a whole wall: cloth notebooks, fountain pens you can try before you buy, bottled ink in twelve colours, and letterpress cards printed a mile from here in the River Arts District.

  • Try before you buyEvery pen is inked and on the counter. Scribble away.
  • Ink refillsBring the bottle back and we fill it for half the price.
  • Cards, one at a timeLetterpress on cotton paper, $6 each, envelope included.
  • Free gift wrappingKraft paper, green ribbon, a handwritten tag if you want one.

About the shop

A small shop, kept by someone who reads everything in it.

I opened Quillmont in the spring of 2016 with eleven hundred books, a borrowed till and a rule that still holds: nothing goes on the shelf that somebody here hasn't read and would press into your hands. That's why our fiction wall is deep rather than wide, why the poetry section is bigger than a town this size has any right to, and why the nature-writing table by the window is the first thing you see when you walk in.

There are four of us now. Jonah runs the kids' corner and the book club, Priya keeps the paper wall stocked and knows every nib by feel, and Sam does the bicycle deliveries. Between us we've read the whole shop, and we'll tell you honestly when something isn't for you.

We don't stock a book because it's selling. We stock it because we'd be embarrassed if you asked and we didn't have it.

— Wren, owner

Two of us, one oven, since 2019.

Jonah does the bread. Priya does the pastry and the coffee. Whoever is less covered in flour talks to you.

We met working the early shift at a bakery in Boston and moved up the coast because we wanted to bake in a neighborhood, not for one. The oven arrived on a flatbed in March 2019 and we’ve lit it every morning since, including the mornings we probably shouldn’t have.

Hollowmere is the name of the pond Jonah grew up next to. The starter is older than the shop. The playlist is Priya’s and not up for discussion.

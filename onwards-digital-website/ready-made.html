Questions people ask

What exactly do I get?

Your finished website, exactly as you designed it here, with your words, photos, colours and fonts, plus clear steps for putting it on your own domain. You choose how it’s sent: a zip file, a single file, or a Google Drive link.

Will anyone else have the same website?

It’s very unlikely. There are over 280 billion ways to combine the designs, colours and fonts before you add a single word or photo of your own.

How do bookings reach me?

You choose. Your site’s forms can use FormSubmit, a free form service, to email each request straight to the address you pick (the first one asks you to confirm it). Or choose to set it up yourself and connect your own booking tool. Onwards Digital never receives, stores or sends your bookings, and we don’t put your site online for you: you own it and run it.

Can I add terms of service?

Yes. Switch on “Terms of service” under Your business and a page appears, linked from your footer, with a starting template you can rewrite. It’s a starting point, not legal advice.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes. Every photo on the site can be replaced from the dropdowns on the right. Until you do, we use licensed example photography.

Would a custom website suit me better?

If you want something designed around your business from scratch, with a free mockup and revisions, a custom build is the better fit. See custom websites.

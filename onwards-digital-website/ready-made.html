Questions people ask

What exactly do I get?

Your finished website, exactly as you designed it here, with your words, photos, colours and fonts, plus clear steps for putting it on your own domain.

Will anyone else have the same website?

It’s very unlikely. There are over 280 billion ways to combine the designs, colours and fonts before you add a single word or photo of your own.

How do bookings reach me?

Every booking and contact form on your site sends straight to the email address you give us. The first time someone books, you’ll get a one-time email asking you to confirm that address.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes. Every photo on the site can be replaced from the dropdowns on the right. Until you do, we use licensed example photography.

Would a custom website suit me better?

If you want something designed around your business from scratch, with a free mockup and revisions, a custom build is the better fit. See custom websites.

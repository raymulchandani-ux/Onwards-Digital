How we work

From the first call to the keys.

  1. One

    A conversation, not a form.

    Forty minutes with one advisor, about how you'll live in the home rather than what it should have. Most people leave the call with a shorter list than they came with.

  2. Two

    Six homes, all of them walked.

    We show you what we'd show a friend. Every home on the shortlist has been visited by someone on the desk, and the notes are yours, including the honest ones.

  3. Three

    Visits arranged around your week.

    Two days in the city, morning and evening light, a driver, and the same advisor throughout. If you're buying abroad, a colleague in that city has already checked the paperwork.

  4. Four

    One fee, agreed before we start.

    A flat fee for the buyer's side, written down at the brief. Lawyers, surveys and currency introduced when you want them, never bundled in.

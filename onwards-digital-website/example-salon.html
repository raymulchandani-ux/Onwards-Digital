II

Four chairs, four specialists.

We don't do faces on the website; we'd rather show you what's on the trolley. Each of us has one thing we're known for. Book by name, or tell us what you're after and we'll put you in the right chair.

01Her shears, not the razor

Dana

Precision cuts

Bobs, blunt lines, grown-out pixies brought back into shape. Dana trained in London and has cut with the shears, never the razor, for twelve years. If you want a line you can see from across the room, this is the chair.

Book with Dana
02Two visits a year

Marisol

Balayage & lived-in colour

Hand-painted blondes and brunettes that grow out without a line. Marisol's aim is that you come in twice a year and look like you didn't. She'll tell you if what you want won't survive Austin sun.

Book with Marisol
03Cut dry, curl by curl

Theo

Curly hair

Every curl cut here is done dry, one curl at a time, so it falls the way you actually wear it rather than the way it looked wet. Theo works types 2A through 4C and will not, under any circumstances, brush it out.

Book with Theo
04Quoted in hours, kept to

Kit

Vivid colour & corrections

Copper, oxblood, silver, blue, and the colours that don't have names yet. Kit is also who you come to when a box dye or a bad bleach went wrong. Corrections are quoted in hours after a consultation, and the quote holds.

Book with Kit

01Every new client starts with a fifteen-minute consultation

It's free and it happens before we book the service, in the chair or over email. We look at your hair, talk about what it does on an ordinary Tuesday, and agree on a plan and a price. For colour, bring a photo or two of what you like, and one of what you don't.

02Twenty-four hours to cancel

Cancel or move your appointment up to twenty-four hours before and there's no charge. Inside that window we charge half the booked service; no-shows are charged in full. A colour appointment holds a chair for three hours, so we hold to this one.

03Parking is behind us

There's a small lot at the back with six spaces marked Tessaline, and the back door is unlocked during hours. South Congress is metered and mostly full by ten, so use the lot. We'll send the way in with your confirmation.

04What to arrive with

For a curly cut, come with your hair down, dry, and styled the way you normally wear it. For everything else, come as you are. Coffee and sparkling water are on us; the espresso machine is the loud thing in the corner.

05Running late, or need to move it?

Reply to your confirmation text, or email hello@tessaline.example. More than fifteen minutes late and we may need to shorten the service or move you to the next opening.

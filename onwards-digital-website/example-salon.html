Before you sit down.

A few things we'd rather you knew now than found out in the chair.

  1. Every new client starts with a fifteen-minute consultation

    It's free and it happens before we book the service, in the chair or over email. We look at your hair, talk about what it does on an ordinary Tuesday, and agree a plan and a price. For colour, bring a photo or two of what you like, and one of what you don't.

  2. Twenty-four hours to cancel

    Cancel or move your appointment up to twenty-four hours before and there's no charge. Inside that window we charge half the booked service; no-shows are charged in full. A colour appointment holds a chair for three hours, so we hold to this one.

  3. Parking is behind us

    There's a small lot at the back with six spaces marked Tessaline, and the back door is unlocked during hours. South Congress is metered and mostly full by ten.

  4. Last colour booking is four o'clock

    Cuts and blowouts run until close. Colour needs the full three hours, so the last colour chair of the day is at four, and Kit's corrections start no later than two.
